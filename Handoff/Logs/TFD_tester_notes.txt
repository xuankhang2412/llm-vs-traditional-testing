2. SPECIFICATION OBSERVATIONS, AMBIGUITIES, AND ASSUMPTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------
a) CSV Header vs. test_id Column:
   The assignment instructions mention 'test_id: T01, T02, ...' alongside 'Do not change the header, the column order or the column names. csv_format.md explains every column.' Neither csv_format.md nor the four attached *_template.csv files include a test_id column. To preserve strict compatibility with your automated grading/mutation harness, the four CSV files strictly retain the exact headers from csv_format.md and the templates (without adding an extra test_id column), while T01..T20 and T01..T35 are documented in order below corresponding to data rows 1..20 and 1..35.

b) Single-Violation Negative Tests (Fault Isolation):
   Every negative test case (expected = ERROR) is constructed to violate exactly ONE domain boundary or business validation rule while keeping all other parameters strictly valid. For example, in Enhanced Package T27 (testing Rule 5: weight > 50.0 lbs with signature = 0), locationType is set to 1 (Residence) and packaging is set to 6 (capacity 60.0 lbs) so that Rule 4 (business signature requirement) and packaging capacity limits are not simultaneously triggered. Similarly, in Enhanced Payroll T25 (testing Rule 5: hourly >= 40.01 with medical = 1), dental is set to 1 so Rule 4 (medical-dental dependency) does not mask a fault in Rule 5.

c) Intermediate Precision and Rounding Policy:
   Per Section 5/6 Clarifications (v1.1) in all four specifications, full numerical precision is preserved across all intermediate steps, and only the final output is rounded to 2 decimal places using standard half-up rounding (ties rounded away from zero, e.g., 12.345 -> 12.35 and -161.795 -> -161.80). Each suite includes dedicated test cases where premature intermediate rounding or banker's rounding (round-half-to-even on an even hundredths digit followed by 5 in the thousandths place, such as 3.125 -> 3.13, 156.465 -> 156.47, 6.125 -> 6.13, and 99.525 -> 99.53) produces a 1-cent difference.

d) Enhanced Package Order of Operations (Packaging Multipliers and Local Discount):
   Per Section 5 of enhanced_package_spec.md, Packaging codes 7 (*1.15) and 8 (*1.30) scale the delivery-multiplied price in Step 3 BEFORE additive service fees are added in Step 4, whereas the Rule 1 local overnight discount (*0.90) is applied in Step 5 to the entire running total AFTER all additive fees.

e) Enhanced Payroll Taxable Bonuses, Pension Cap, Subsidies, and Credits:
   Per Section 4 and Section 5 of enhanced_payroll_spec.md:
   - Overtime (> 5.0 hrs -> +$50.00) and Holiday (> 5.0 hrs -> +$75.00) bonuses are added to grossPay in Step 1, meaning all taxes (Federal, Social Security, Medicare, State) and the 10% pension deduction cap (0.10 * grossPay) are calculated on grossPay inclusive of bonuses.
   - Under Rule 6 (hourly <= $20.00), medical, dental, and vision deductions are subsidized to $0.00, but Rule 4 (medical != 0 requires dental != 0) still applies as an enrollment validation rule (tested in T23), and Rule 7 (+$15.00 wellness credit for Medical 3 + Dental 2 + Vision 2) operates independently of the Rule 6 subsidy (tested in T03).
